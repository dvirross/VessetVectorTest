# Literature search log — prior randomisation-based tests of vesset pattern frequencies

**Claim being checked (manuscript, Section 1):** we are not aware of any formal
randomisation-based (permutation / Monte Carlo) test of whether the frequency of
halachic vesset-type arithmetic patterns in empirical menstrual-cycle data departs from
a chance null.

**Screening criterion:** a hit counts as contradicting the claim only if it (a) uses
empirical cycle data, (b) defines rule-based arithmetic events (equal intervals,
constant first or second differences, weekly anchors, or equivalent), and (c) compares
their observed frequency with a randomisation, permutation, bootstrap or Monte Carlo
null. Descriptive halachic studies, forecasting models, Markov models and regularity
statistics do not count.

## Stage 1 — author's search during the dissertation (2019–2022)

Unstructured search of the Hebrew and English halachic and medical-halachic
literature and of the biostatistical menstrual-cycle literature, carried out while
writing the dissertation (Ross, 2022, Ariel University) and the B.D.D. article
(accepted 2021). No study meeting the criterion was found. The quantitative
halachic works located (the author's own Markov-chain and reliability studies, and
the abstinence-rules analysis) are cited in Section 2.3 of the manuscript; none tests
pattern frequencies against a randomisation null.

**Result:** no publication meeting the criterion was found in either stage.

**Limitations:** Stage 1 was not protocol-driven and its queries were not logged.
Stage 2 used a general web index rather than bibliographic databases, screened
snippets rather than full texts, and cannot rule out untranslated or unindexed
Hebrew material (e.g., in *Techumin*, *Assia*, *B.D.D.*) beyond what Stage 1 covered.
The manuscript therefore keeps "we are not aware of" rather than asserting absence.
